About the system

The TESDA Lucena Batch Availability System helps trainees see open training batches and enroll online.

Frequently asked questions

Who can enroll?

Anyone who creates an account and meets the course requirements.

How do I know my enrollment is confirmed?

You will receive a notification and your dashboard will show "Enrollment Confirmed".

Can I withdraw from a course?

Yes. Open My Enrollment and choose Withdraw. You can then join another batch.

What should I bring on the first day?

A valid ID and your enrollment form.

Ready to enroll?

Log in or create an account to see available batches.
